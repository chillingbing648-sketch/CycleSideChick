# Feature Matrix: Flo vs. CycleSideChick

Competitive research analysis comparing Flo Health (as documented on flo.health, App Store, Google Play, and public documentation) with CycleSideChick v2.0.0.

| Flo Feature | CycleSideChick Today | Gap | Decision | User Value | Privacy / Safety Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Period & Cycle Prediction** | Recency-weighted mean of last 6 cycles with median outlier fallback, ±SD range capped at ±7, confidence score with clinical reasons. | Minor; Flo uses a proprietary multi-layer neural network trained on millions of users. | **Build** | Accurate, personalized period prediction that adapts to cycle history without overfitting. | Low risk: 100% on-device local calculation avoids exposing sensitive cycle length data to remote servers. |
| **Ovulation & Fertile Window Estimation** | Configurable luteal length (10–16d, default 14d). Estimated ovulation = predicted start - luteal length; estimated window = ovulation -5 to +1. | Flo integrates proprietary probability algorithms and dynamic likelihood curves. | **Build** | Clear, transparent understanding of fertile timing for family planning or cycle awareness. | Safety risk: Users might mistakenly treat estimates as contraception. Mitigated with clear disclaimers that calendar predictions are never contraception. |
| **Symptom & Mood Logging** | 6 default categories + custom symptoms with 3-level intensity (mild, moderate, severe), 5 expressive mood states, 0–10 pain slider, energy, sleep hours & quality, 8-glass water tracker, medication log. | Flo tracks 70+ categorized symptoms, bowel habits, discharge, sex drive, and physical sensations. | **Build** | Comprehensive daily wellness logging to identify personal cycle correlations. | Privacy risk: Granular lifestyle data is sensitive. Mitigated by storing strictly in browser local storage with zero cloud transmission. |
| **BBT & LH Surge Testing** | Basal body temperature (BBT) waking temperature input and LH ovulation test status (none, negative, faint, positive/peak). | Flo provides graphical biphasic temperature chart overlays and camera-assisted test strip scanning. | **Build** | Empowers users attempting natural conception (TTC) to confirm ovulation biphasic shifts. | Low risk: On-device verification prevents fertility intent profiling by data brokers. |
| **Cycle Insights & Health Reports** | Interactive cycle length history chart, symptom frequency by cycle phase, period length & variation metrics, "Compare to your usual" baseline. | Flo offers extensive AI-generated insight cards, monthly pattern recaps, and statistical trend correlations. | **Build** | Visual feedback on hormonal trends and early detection of cycle irregularity. | Low risk: Computed entirely locally on client CPU with zero server telemetry. |
| **Local Health Assistant** | Rule-based, on-device Q&A engine with 7+ clinical topics, suggested prompt chips, peer-reviewed medical citations, and clinician advice paths. | Flo features an automated interactive virtual chatbot ("Flo Health Assistant") powered by cloud servers. | **Build** | Instant, non-diagnostic answers to common questions (late period, cramps, discharge) grounded in ACOG/NHS guidance. | Safety risk: Risk of user confusing advice with formal diagnosis. Mitigated with non-diagnostic wording and immediate "consult a clinician" pathways. |
| **Content & Education Library** | 22 original peer-reviewed articles covering cycle phases, hormones, dysmenorrhea, contraception, perimenopause, and pelvic health with formal clinical citations. | Flo provides hundreds of articles, video courses, expert webinars, and audio guides behind a paid subscription ($50+/yr). | **Build** | Free, accessible, evidence-based reproductive literacy without paywalls or tracking cookies. | Safety risk: Inaccurate medical advice. Mitigated by citing only peer-reviewed consensus (ACOG, NHS, WHO, NICE, NAMS, Cochrane). |
| **Clinician Report Export** | Formatted printable and copyable plain-language summary detailing cycle lengths, period durations, symptom frequencies, pain ratings, and active clinical triggers. | Flo offers a PDF "Doctor Report" summarising user data for healthcare appointments. | **Build** | Facilitates informed, collaborative discussions between users and their healthcare providers. | Privacy risk: Exported text could be accidentally shared. Mitigated by keeping export user-initiated and client-side only. |
| **Pregnancy Mode** | Gestational age calculator (weeks & days) from LMP using Naegele's rule, trimester tracking, developmental milestones, calm guidance. Pauses cycle predictions. | Flo offers week-by-week fetal development illustrations, contraction timer, kick counter, and weight tracker. | **Build** | Calm, supportive milestone tracking during pregnancy without alarming false period overdue alerts. | Safety risk: Mistaking gestational calculators for ultrasound dating. Mitigated by noting ultrasound confirmation as medical standard. |
| **Perimenopause Mode** | Cycle variability tracking, vasomotor symptom logging (hot flashes, night sweats), sleep disruption, relief options, and non-rigid ovulation logic. | Flo features "Flo for Perimenopause" with Peri Score and symptom burden surveys based on the Menopause Rating Scale. | **Build** | Validates and tracks hormonal shifts in women in their 40s without forcing rigid regular cycle assumptions. | Low risk: Normalizes transition symptoms and flags when bleeding changes warrant clinical investigation. |
| **Anonymous Mode / Privacy Stance** | 100% on-device architecture, zero accounts, zero trackers, zero analytics, privacy ledger, salted PIN lock, and cryptographic wipe. | Flo uses "Anonymous Mode" via Oblivious HTTP relays to decouple identity from cloud accounts; standard mode historically involved third-party marketing SDKs. | **Build** | True absolute privacy: mathematical impossibility of remote data subpoena, breach, or commercial sale. | Safety risk: If user loses device without exporting backup, data cannot be recovered remotely. Mitigated with clear CSV/JSON export prompts. |
| **Partner Sharing** | Read-only, opt-in partner snapshot generated locally as text or encrypted link snippet with zero server accounts; instantly revocable. | "Flo for Partners" connects a partner's separate smartphone app account via cloud sync to view cycle phases and pregnancy progress. | **Adapt** | Helps partners provide empathetic support and understand cycle phases without requiring an account. | Privacy risk: Partner access could be coerced. Mitigated by keeping share opt-in, read-only, non-cloud, and locally revocable at any time. |
| **Reminders & Push Alerts** | In-app notification checks; service worker push registration; discreet notification wording option ("Update from Bun"). | Flo sends customizable push notifications for periods, ovulation, pills, and water intake via Firebase/APNs cloud servers. | **Adapt** | Gentle reminders to log symptoms and prepare supplies before periods start. | Privacy risk: Lock screen notifications exposing reproductive state. Mitigated with optional discreet notification copy. |
| **Social / Community Chat** | Not implemented. | Flo features "Secret Chats" where users discuss topics anonymously in moderated forums. | **Skip** | User value: peer support. | Privacy/Safety risk: Requires cloud servers, identity tokens, moderation infrastructure, and creates significant privacy risk of peer-to-peer data exposure. |
